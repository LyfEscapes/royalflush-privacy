Royal Flush: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Publisher: t0ilet b0wl gaMez (LyfEscapes LLC) · Contact: support@lyfescapes.net

This policy explains what information the Royal Flush mobile game ("the game") uses. We designed the game to need as little personal information as possible.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts, sign-ups or social features. We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, boosters, settings and purchases you have made) is stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads and, depending on your region and choices, to personalize them. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). In regions that require it, the game shows a consent form before personalized ads. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this game, and does not receive your payment card details, which stay with Google or Apple. See RevenueCat's policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob, Google Play / Apple App Store, RevenueCat) to provide ads and purchases.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold an account for you, there is no account to delete. To manage ad data, use your device's ad settings or the privacy controls offered by Google. To ask us anything about this policy, contact us at the email above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held by the service providers listed above, which you can exercise with them directly.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and publish the new version here.
